## All MusicPi Repos:

### Homepage:
[MusicPI-Home](https://github.com/JumesP/musicpi-home) - entry point (desktop of sorts) displays currently playing from both media types. app selection, aesthetic backgrounds

### Spotify:
[Cadence](https://github.com/JumesP/cadence) - Makes requests to spotify API, handles displaing, selection and control of the spotify music.

Spotify Soloist - plays the spotify music on the device.

### Local Playback:
[playback-engine](https://github.com/JumesP/playback-engine) - plays the local playback on the device.

[music-library](https://github.com/JumesP/music-library) - handles displaying, selection and queueing of the local music
